Controls. Click the walkthrough to capture the mouse, then W A S D to walk and the mouse to look. Esc releases the pointer. Views opens saved shots, Plan shows the floor plan, Measured overlays the derived shell. Search any of the 194 places to route to it.

Browsers. Chrome or Edge on desktop for the full walkthrough; Safari on macOS and iOS loads the model and the capture pages. AR navigation needs WebXR, which today means Android Chrome. Nothing needs an install or an account.

5 · A five-minute tour for reviewers

  1. Open ricco.codes/ply and scroll through the film, or press Skip to the end to reach the entry card.
  2. Start a capture walks the capture flow; Explore the demo space opens the twin.
  3. In the walkthrough, click once to take the mouse, then W A S D to walk. Plan gives the floor plan, Dollhouse the cutaway.
  4. Search a place by name and follow the route. The line bends around desks, not through them.
  5. On Android Chrome, the AR button places the same route on the real floor with two taps to align.

6 · Reproducing a room

python -m plypipe run <folder of clips> --out work/my-room --preset standard
# writes scene.json, shell.json, places.json, scene.spz, scene_lite.spz, report.json
# then open walk.html?scene=<url of scene.json>

Settings are planned from the capture, so there is nothing to tune between rooms. docs/PROCESSING.md explains what is measured and why, including the post-run checklist.
