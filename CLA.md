## How to sign

Add a comment to your first pull request containing exactly this line, with your details:

```
I have read the CLA and I hereby sign it. Name: <full legal name>, GitHub: @<handle>, Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
```

The Owner records that comment with the pull request. If You contribute on behalf of a company,
add the company name and the signatory's title.
