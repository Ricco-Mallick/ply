Ply · capture tutorial

Film it like you'd mow a lawn.

A reel mower cuts only while its wheels turn, and every pass has to lap the one before it — miss the overlap and a strip is left standing. A room is the same: walk slowly, hold the phone level, and let each pass overlap the last. What you skip is what the twin will have a hole in.

Slow, not clever

The reel only cuts while the wheels turn. In the capture, motion blur is what breaks frames — half your walking speed doubles the sharp frames the solver can use.

Overlap each pass

Lap the previous pass by about a third. The matcher needs shared views between passes to stitch them into one model; a gap becomes a hole you can see straight through.

Keep it level

Hold the phone upright and at the same height. The pipeline's scale comes from that height, so a bouncing hand makes the room the wrong size.

Corners last

Do the walls first, then step into each corner and turn slowly. Corners are where the model folds, and a fold is the one failure retraining cannot fix.
