PLY

One phone walkthrough becomes a photoreal 3D twin with searchable rooms and walking directions around real obstacles.

Experience with headphones
01 / Capture

Every frame you keep becomes a camera in space. Ply coaches you live until every wall, corner and doorway is covered.

02 / Reconstruct

Cameras are solved, then millions of gaussians are trained to reproduce what the phone saw. Exposure flicker is corrected on the way.

03 / Twin

Floors are levelled, walls found and the walkable area extracted. Every distance is in real metres.

04 / Explore

A real chapel scan, replayed along the path the scanner walked. Geometry in the browser, not a video.

05 / Label

AI finds rooms, doors and objects and reads the signs on the doors. People confirm, the twin learns the place.

06 / Navigate

Search any room or object. The route stays on the floor and bends around tables, seats and walls.

07 / Arrive

Scan the marker at the entrance. Arrows appear on the real floor and update as you walk.
